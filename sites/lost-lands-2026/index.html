Install on your phone (works offline once added)

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the ••• button next to the address bar (on older iPhones, tap the Share button at the bottom instead).
  3. Tap Share, then scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen (tap View More first if you don't see it).
  4. Leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app if Chrome offers it directly), then confirm.

Pick your favorites from the home-screen app itself, not the browser tab — the two don't share saved picks. Then, while you still have signal, open the app once and tap through Lineup, My Schedule, and Map so everything's cached for when service at the venue gets spotty.
